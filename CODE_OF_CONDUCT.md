# Code of Conduct

AlgoWise is a learning project. Many contributors are students writing their first pull request, and we want it to stay welcoming.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent and explain your reasoning.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Welcome questions. There are no silly questions about algorithms.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of learning.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexual or demeaning language, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock comments, issues and pull requests that break these standards, and may temporarily or permanently remove contributors. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private message or the email listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and events where someone represents the project.
